  <h1>About</h1>

  <p>Hey, I'm Kishore.</p>

  <p>I'm currently pursuing my Master's in Computer Science at the University of Southern California. Before USC, I spent several years as a software engineer at Microsoft and Apple, with an earlier stint at Cisco.</p>

  <p>My work has largely been around <strong>distributed systems, backend infrastructure, databases, and cloud platforms</strong>. At Microsoft, I've been working on the infrastructure behind <strong>agentic AI systems and LLM-powered applications</strong>, with a focus on the systems and platform layer that makes these applications reliable and scalable. At Apple, I worked across <strong>cloud and network infrastructure</strong>, building systems for large-scale network operations and automation. My earlier work at Cisco introduced me to <strong>distributed data systems and analytics</strong>.</p>

  <p>Over time, my interests have converged around the intersection of <strong>distributed systems, databases, software architecture, and AI</strong> — particularly the challenges that emerge when intelligent, increasingly autonomous applications meet the infrastructure underneath them.</p>

  <p>This blog is where I explore those ideas. Some posts come from problems I've encountered while building production systems; others come from research papers, coursework, or things I'm experimenting with. I use it mostly as a place to understand things more deeply and share what I learn along the way.</p>

  <p>If you're interested in systems, AI infrastructure, databases, or the interesting problems at their intersection, you're probably in the right place.</p>

  <p>You can find me on <a href="https://github.com/Kishore712" target="_blank">GitHub</a> and <a href="https://linkedin.com/in/" target="_blank">LinkedIn</a>.</p>
